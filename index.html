Chat Transfers

LiveChat only — a chat counts as "Transferred" if more than one agent sent a message in it. Shares the same cache as Total Chats, so it's already backfilled/updated there.

Monthly Summary

Department cards count each chat once, toward whichever department it ended with (a chat transferred General → Social Trade only counts for Social Trade). The employee table's Total Chats/% Share instead counts every chat an employee ever touched (matches Total Chats/Transfers), so the two totals won't always match — that's expected. Leave days come from the Leave sheet configured in Config.

Supervised Chats

Chats where someone other than the assigned agent left a private note/annotation — i.e. a supervisor had to step in and guide them.
